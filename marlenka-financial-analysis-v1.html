The Marlenka Case: A Sweet Financial Analysis

People in 50 countries know the honey cake from Frýdek-Místek. But how is the company behind it doing? Explore its real financial statements for 2021–2024, calculate five key ratios and solve three mysteries.

Step 1 · Meet the company

Who is Marlenka?

Your task

Become a financial analyst

A financial analyst reads a company’s financial statements to find out how it is doing: whether it makes a profit, whether it can pay its debts and how it is financed. But first, try to guess what Marlenka looks like.

Who it is
MARLENKA international s.r.o.

Company ID 25900706 · Frýdek-Místek, Czech Republic · family business founded in 2003

The story

Founder Gevorg Avetisjan came from Armenia and started baking a honey cake from a family recipe. Today the company has more than 340 employees and over 50 products.

Where it sells

To 46 countries in Europe, North America, Asia and Australia. Exports account for 72% of revenue.

News in 2024

The company bought the site of a former textile factory. It plans to build a new production plant, a Marlenka museum and a café there.
